# Native map-probe calibration protocol — 2026-09-06

This package freezes a sealed collision-only map and nine ordered mapbench inputs before testing a revised native support query. The geometry is analytic and independent of the Kejim and Dantooine navmeshes. Its purpose is to distinguish query artifacts from known brush facts; it is not evidence about real-map navigation or movement.

The map contains six isolated point cases: a WORLD floor with a tall ceiling, a valid 72-unit floor-to-ceiling space, an obstructed 56-unit space, a ledge whose only floor is below the query window, a declared surface 64 units above its actual WORLD floor, and a simple wedge rising 32 units over 192. Three short sweeps cover the tall floor, valid low ceiling, and unsupported ledge. The standing hull is 30 by 30 by 64 units and its native standing center is 24.125 units above the floor. Thus 72 units is valid with modest overhead clearance, while 56 intersects the hull.

The original mapbench floor sweep starts a standing hull at declared surface Z + 88 and extends its top to surface Z + 128. It should accept the tall bay, but its start overlaps both low ceilings. `floor_solid` in those bays is an expected property of that old query construction, not evidence that the 72-unit bay lacks standing room. The 56-unit bay must still fail clearance under a revised query. The ledge must lack support in the bounded floor window, and the displaced declaration must fail the 24-unit surface association rule.

A revised query should first project a native floor without sweeping a full body downward through overhead geometry. It must then test the stationary standing hull and a short downward standing-hull support query. That final support query must independently require finite results, WORLD support, a walkable normal, and safe contents. A point normal alone does not establish radius-wide hull support. Sweep cases remain endpoint support plus direct hull sweep; they do not establish continuous ground, jump feasibility, or transition validity.

The artifact binds and snapshots the generator, this protocol, imported geometry/checksum helpers, compiler, generated MAP/BSP, compile log, exact ordered payload, and analytic expectations. Native outcomes are added later by an isolated runner. Every mismatch is retained rather than changing an expectation after observation.

## Frozen version 2 query declaration

`BOT2MAPPROBE1` retains the original high-start standing-hull floor sweep and its existing result meanings. `BOT2MAPPROBE2` first projects a zero-size point from declared surface Z + 24.125 down to surface Z - 104. The point result must be finite, WORLD-owned, at least 0.7 in normal Z, and within 24 units of the declared surface. This lower window is intentionally unchanged from version 1 so displaced-floor controls remain height-association failures rather than becoming shorter-window misses.

For a valid projected plane, version 2 positions the standing AABB using `24.125 + 15 * (abs(nx) + abs(ny)) / nz` above the projected point. This accounts for the lowest AABB corner on a slope. A native 0.5-unit downward full-hull trace then independently requires finite WORLD support and a walkable normal. The resolved center is lifted 0.125 units and receives an unconditional stationary-hull occupancy trace, including when the short support trace reports solid or missing support. Existing liquid, hurt, mover, saturation, height, and direct sweep checks remain fail-closed.

The result wire shape is unchanged. `START version=2` binds interpretation. For version 2, `fraction` is the zero-size projection fraction; `floor`, `type`, and `normal` describe the full-hull support trace when that trace was attempted, otherwise the rejected point projection. `clear` describes the independently measured stationary occupancy after a finite support trace. An S record combines both endpoint reasons and the direct resolved-center hull sweep. It still does not prove continuous grounded travel or jump feasibility.
