# Native empirical-coverage probe — 2026-09-06

## Scope

This experiment moves the nominal-map empirical coverage selector into an opt-in native fixture path. It tests whether a table baked from audited full searches can shortlist at most two candidates after twelve native prefix steps per proposal, then run the existing four-second selected diagnostic forecast before any live movement. The selected forecast scores and records the fixed choice; it cannot choose or veto a different candidate.

The coverage cells are empirical corner observations, not certified continuous funnels. The probe does not change production dispatch, prove a final connector, measure M3 pass cost, or support active dynamic bodies.

## Frozen stages

Training uses 27 fresh full-eight continuation searches at `x ∈ {-24,0,24}`, `y ∈ {-16,0,16}`, `vy ∈ {-50,0,50}`, with `z=24.125`, `vx=0`, and yaw zero. The bake stage independently audits every raw capture, builds the existing maneuver library, and compiles the eight-cell coverage artifact. Library and coverage bytes are frozen before evaluation.

Evaluation uses 16 new roots that were not used by the earlier shadow campaign: `x ∈ {-21,-9,9,21}`, `y ∈ {-12,12}`, and `vy ∈ {-37,37}`. Each root has a full-eight reference arm and a native `--maneuver-coverage` arm. Both arms run 160 live native steps after their guarded selection work.

Four native outside-domain controls set one field to `x=-25`, `x=25`, `y=-17`, or `vy=51`, with other coordinates and velocities zero. They must log `COVERAGE_MISS reason=outside_domain calls=0` and finish as `coverage_miss` before live movement. Two binding controls flip one bit in the frozen coverage map hash or candidate hash; both must miss with zero prefix calls and no live movement. A terminal-envelope control shifts every proposal's X envelope by +1000; it may consume at most 24 prefix steps and must miss before live movement.

An optional timing stage runs five serialized native coverage cases only after outcome collection. It measures a quiet repeated prefix-selection path with fixture logging excluded from the measured span. Timing is reported independently and cannot convert this probe into a production-cost or M3 claim.

Every case, control, port, root, mutation, command, build source, binary, helper, protocol, geometry, BSP, library, and coverage artifact is declared and hashed before the corresponding native stage. Stages use fresh output directories, retain every failure, and run at most four outcome worlds. Timing worlds run one at a time.

## Audit contract

All positive worlds must independently bind the staged executable, DLL, BSP, loaded module, source manifest, exact actual root and velocity, pending-contact acquisition, physics, geometry, and 160 ordered 25 ms movement steps. Raw captures must equal saved decoded JSON canonically and pass all four movement parity gates with zero unsupported records.

The full-search arm must reproduce all eight native candidates. The coverage arm must verify the exact encoded coverage bytes, cell and root compatibility, first-twelve-step prefix cadence and roots, at most two proposals and 24 prefix pmove calls, the selected ID, the subsequent full selected diagnostic forecast, and 160 live steps. Prefix evaluation and full validation counts remain separate.

Both arms are hypothesized to reach a useful exit in all 16 paired roots. The audit reports finish time, fixed-window walking, idle, airborne time, horizontal travel, and longest walking and idle spans. Four-second episodes cannot test the five-second confinement gate and cannot establish absence of stuck behavior.

Outside-domain, binding, and terminal-envelope controls are evidence-valid refusals. They are not silently excluded or reclassified as native launch failures. No control may fall back to an undeclared full search.

This probe covers one static known map and exact decoded compatibility fields. Dynamic obstacles, active bodies, resource affordability, approximate matching, other maps, live recovery after refusal, pass cost, and production integration remain unqualified.

## Consequence for the active plan

Keep full actual-state continuation validation as the reliable reference. The
new native path remains explicitly opt-in research and is not installed in the
game. Do not turn the [-33,-32] range into [-33,-31] to obtain a pass: that would
leave the accepted clearance failure untouched.

Before another acceptance attempt, make preparation search interior and adverse
entry states automatically and retain failures with the successful policies.
Use physical landing/continuation slack in selection and optimization, then
refine or refuse regions with contradictory outcomes. Evaluate the resulting
regions on new states. Also separate required prediction/controller work from
diagnostic hashing/export cost while preserving the guards, so affordability
can be improved without relying on an invalid early acceptance rule.
