# A measured stand-time improvement, 2026-09-20

Eight fresh, serial, counterbalanced pairs show a benchmark speed win on
`red_out_L1_stand`: median **18,887.5 → 17,900 ms**, mean **19,346.875 →
18,493.75 ms**, maximum **20,750 → 20,275 ms**. Six pairs improve and two
regress. This is a 5.23% median and 4.41% mean improvement in this configuration,
not attainment of the roughly 15-second target or production qualification.

## Implementation and configuration

The native change permits existing arc-aimed proposals at an observed final
flight landing even when the next straight endpoint is under 192 units away.
It retains the valid-path and 128-unit goal-distance gates. Ordinary walking
keeps its minimum length; native fall, trigger, path, onward and walking-margin
checks are unchanged. The reproduced landing was 131.35 units before a
24.70-degree bend: `StraightAhead` stops there, while `PathPointAt` can propose
a continuation through it. Offering a candidate does not admit it.

Both confirmation arms use that same changed build. The winning arm enables
the existing **`bot_moveReroute=1` and `bot_moveExitSpeed=2000`** together;
the control uses zero for both. Other explicit settings are
`bot_moveStatePrefer=1`, `bot_moveHeadingSweep=0`, `bot_moveReplay=0`.
These two experimental settings remain default off. Nothing is installed.
The result establishes the combination, not the isolated effect of either
setting or of the short-landing change.

All worlds use the existing `move-explore-20260916-b/seed.txt`, no human
corridor or playback, `--edges --parity 256 --momentum-trace`, timescale 5,
and the same fixed 1 / msec 8 / float 1 / fps 40 native physics. The case starts
stationary at the red stand with Force 0, permits no Force movement spend,
and leaves native regeneration intact. Every world has its own homepath and
port. Timing worlds run alone; odd pairs run control first, even pairs credit
first. Screening is excluded from confirmation.

## What the logs establish

In screening pairs 1 and 2, control and credit have identical logged states
through relative 8,675 ms. At the 388-unit/sec landing near (1013, 1732, 1808),
three two-hop immediate candidates change from selected-path rejection to
validated rerouted continuations. One-hop alternatives remain refused. The
control selects a 24-unit run-up; credit selects an immediate tap with its own
validated 10-cell route. The chosen score includes margin 264.6 and exit credit
950.6. At the next frame the control is grounded at 298.7 units/sec, while the
candidate is airborne at 388. Both admission and scoring are active, but these
observations do not isolate their individual effects or assign the entire
trip saving to this one landing.

The short-landing change alone has a smaller confirmed result: mean
20,243.75 → 19,984.375 ms, median 20,675 → 20,475, unchanged maximum 20,750.
Two matched logged prefixes show an immediate 445.7-unit/sec tap where the
control waits 350 ms before committing a run-up. Later trajectories diverge.
That marginal aggregate gain was not accepted as the stopping point.

## Safety, cost and limits

The winning configuration passes the **36-case unseeded floor** and both seeded
named regressions, `blue_out_L1_stand` and `red_ret_L1_stand`, with zero deaths
and movement ownership only. Those outcome runs use at most four isolated
worlds; their timings are not evidence of speed. Level 2/3 floor cases retain
their declared Force settings; only level 1 is the no-spend comparison.
The changed build also passes **36/36 with both experimental settings off**,
with zero deaths and movement ownership only. In total, both floors and the
named regressions are 74/74; the default floor has 5,509 parity refusals and
zero comparisons. The extracted-C gate regression passes its bounds and the
old gate fails the retained short-landing case.

Parity remains unqualified: confirmation has **zero compared boundaries**,
zero mismatches and 1,357/1,358 refusals; the candidate floor has 5,491 refusals
and the two named cases 278, also with zero comparisons. No trigger or pit
gate was weakened. A residual short-landing skip also within the goal cutoff
can still be labeled `straight_too_short` by the existing diagnostic ternary;
the goal restriction remains enforced.

Recorded per-search rollout cost increases: mean 15.96 → 22.68 ms, median
15.3 → 22.15, p90 25.1 → 37.3 and maximum 43 → 54 ms in the safe outbound
windows. This is fixture telemetry, not a controlled production CPU benchmark.
The speed improvement buys more search work. Runtime scheduling, multi-bot
cost, positive forecast parity and ordinary multiplayer enablement stay open.
