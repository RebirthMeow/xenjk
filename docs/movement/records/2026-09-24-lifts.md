# Lifts: resting movers as floor, and rides, 2026-09-24

Owner order: lifts next (the handoff's first open item). Step 1 is the
handoff's "stationary movers as floor in the graph and forecast"; step 2 is
riding. Owner guidance given after step 1: jumping down is fine and usually
faster, as long as the path is clear and the fall damage does not kill; riding
up is what is usually needed, and a failed ride up means the lift comes down on
the bot.

## The lifts on ctf4

Six `func_door` lifts (`*12 *14 *16 *17 *19 *21`). Each is `angle -2`,
`spawnflags 5` (start open, crusher), speed 500, `wait 3`, `dmg 5000`.
- **Where they rest.** Start open and moving down, a lift spawns at the bottom
  (`MOVER_POS1`). There its top is 8 units above the lower floor, so it can be
  stepped onto.
- **Travel.** 376 units (`*12`, `*21`) or 624-628 units (the other four).
- **The calls.** Each lift has two `trigger_multiple` calls.
  - Top: `delay "0.2"`, which `G_SpawnInt` reads as 0, so it fires at once.
  - Bottom (on the platform): delay 2 s.
  - After either one fires, the door's own delay applies. Raised, the lift
    waits 3 s and returns (`ReturnToPos1`).

## What was built (worktree `../xenjk-lifts`, branch `movement/lifts`)

- **`bot2_mover.c`**: the rest time of a binary mover
  (`reached == Reached_BinaryMover`).
  - It is floor only while it rests at `POS1` or `POS2` with a stationary
    trajectory. It stays floor until the earliest of three things:
    - its own scheduled start (`Use_BinaryMover_Go`);
    - its return (`ReturnToPos1`; a toggle's -1 means never);
    - any `trigger_multiple` already counting down to use it (`multi_trigger_run`).
  - Spawn-time thinks move nothing and are ignored.
  - The value is an absolute time, so it holds inside a rollout's simulated
    frames.
  - The lift-call table from `bot2_forecast.c` moved here and now carries each
    call's delay: the trigger's delay plus the door's.
- **Forecast.** Any mover contact used to refuse a rollout (`BODY`). Now
  contact with a resting mover, or standing on one, is refused only once the
  simulated time reaches its rest time. A lift call the rollout touches brings
  that time forward to the touch plus the call's delays.
  - The calls are recorded per rollout on every step, replayed steps included.
  - Maps with no lift calls skip the scan.
- **Graph and walk.**
  - `Bot2_GroundSample` admits a resting mover's floor, so the walk and the
    graph fill admit it identically.
  - A cell on such a floor records the lift's inline model and resting
    position.
  - `Graph_CellStands` stops both searches from entering the cell unless that
    lift rests there now.
  - World cells serialize exactly as before, so a graph with no lift cells
    keeps its cache text and identity. Kejim's seed is still
    `g1-98eb1e97c24a4e21`.
  - The report gains `mover_cells=`. ctf4 has 192; Kejim and Dantooine have 0.
- **Live checks.** Fifteen checks that ground means `ENTITYNUM_WORLD` now
  accept a resting lift (`Bot2_StandableEntity`): eight in `bot2_move.c`
  (including the knock-recovery admission), three in `bot2_flight.c`, two in
  `bot2_flightplan.c`, two in `bot2_path.c`.

Build `lift-build-20260924-a` had a zero-initialized memo. At map load
`level.time` and `level.startTime` are both 0, so every lift read as not
floor. Its campaign is kept as `lift-20260924-a-invalid`. Build `b` fixed it,
and `Bot2_MoverReset` runs at every map load.

## Step 2: rides, a lethal-fall refusal, trigger re-arm

Build `lift-build-20260924-d` (worktree `../xenjk-lifts`),
`.codex-research/lift-20260924-d`. Build `c` (rides up only, smoke
`lift-smoke-20260924-c`) came first.

- **Lift edges** (`GRAPH_EDGE_LIFT`, `bot2_graph.c` `Lifts`; the edge's `tap`
  holds the lift's inline model).
  - Up: from the boarding cell, the lift cell nearest the platform's middle,
    to each world cell beside the raised platform. A call must reach the
    boarding cell (`Bot2_MoverBoardDelay`).
  - Down: from the world cell inside a call nearest the raised boarding point
    (within 64 units of its height) to each world cell beside the resting
    platform.
  - Cost: the call's delay, the travel at the lift's speed, the wait at the top
    for a ride down, 500 ms a stop, and the walk to the exit.
  - ctf4: all six lifts, 7-16 up and 6-10 down exits each. Priced against
    climbs (about 2.1 s for a 384-unit climb against 3.3-3.8 s for a ride),
    the planner still climbs when it can.
- **The ride** (`bot2_move.c` `LiftRide`, after knock handling in
  `Move_Command`; one state, `movement_t.lift`, reset by `Move_Reset`).
  - Up: board, holding on the boarding point until the call the bot stands in
    starts the lift; ride, standing still; exit, walking to the landing once
    the lift rests at the top.
  - Down: call, holding in the call until the lift rests at the top; board,
    stepping onto its middle and staying until it leaves; then ride and exit.
  - Each phase has a bound. Leaving the lift, missing it, no start, or no exit
    is a miss charged to the edge. A completed ride sets the edge's cost.
  - Ride edges are left out of the rollout edge search, as pads are.
- **Safety under the lift.** The planner enters lift cells only while the lift
  rests where they were sampled. With the lift away, the walk refuses the
  empty shaft as a 24-unit drop. So the bot is never routed under a lift that
  could come down.
- **Lethal falls** (`BOT2_FORECAST_FALL_DAMAGE`). A rollout adds up the damage
  `ClientEvents` would deal for its `EV_FALL`/`EV_ROLL` events: Pmove's delta,
  already reduced for Force jumps and rolls, times 0.16 above 44, or times 1
  in a knockdown. It is refused only when the total reaches the bot's health,
  so any drop that does not kill stays allowed. The formula matches
  `g_active.c`; no world in these runs reached the refusal.
- **Trigger re-arm.** A call touched during its wait, or while its fire is
  pending, does not fire (`multi_trigger`), and the forecast now agrees. A
  drop through a recently fired call no longer assumes the lift rises.
- **Exit fix (build `d`).** An exit cell can overlap a raised lift's bounds
  (ctf4 *17). In build `c`, 24 of 36 exits there timed out on the lift beside
  their landing. Any standable floor at the landing now completes the ride.

## Open

- The lethal-fall refusal is unmeasured in play; it needs a low-health case.
- A bot that stops on a bottom platform off its ride is carried up 2.2 s later.
  The forecast accounts for it; the walk does not avoid it. Not seen.
- Rides are built only for binary movers resting at `POS1` when the map
  loads, with one call cell a ride down. Trains, plats with their own touch
  triggers, and lifts called only by buttons are not handled.
- A called lift that does not arrive (another player holding it, a call in
  its re-arm) ends the ride as a miss after 8 s; two misses disable the edge.
