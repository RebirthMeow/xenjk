# Milestone B, step B1a: the first camper (2026-10-02)

Plan: [MILESTONE_B.md](../MILESTONE_B.md). Build `camper-build-20261002-c`, `.codex-research/camper-20261002-a/`
(`run.sh`, `summ.py`, `summary.txt`). Kejim, milestone A defaults and the same defender arms as
`protect-20261002-a/run2.sh`; 8 laps worlds of ten game minutes an arm: runner alone (`nc`) and runner + camper (`ca`,
`defense_laps.py --camper`). Every world distinct, no crashes.

## What was built

- `Camper_v2` (`adddefender <team> camper`, bot2_defender.c): slot 2 of the defender module, a guest mover with the
  runner's light loadout (`kejim_runner.jkb`) and the defenders' weapons. Goals, first that applies: carry the enemy
  flag home; retake it where it lies dropped on the graph; take it from its stand while the own runner is dead (kept
  until taken); else camp at `bot_camperPost` and fight. CAMPER lines log each goal change.
- **Target choice for every role** (`ChooseTarget`): the enemy carrying the own flag, else the current target for at
  least 1 s or while a throw at it is followed, else the visible enemy nearest the own carrier (or itself). A new target
  restarts the perception history. With one enemy (every world before the camper) the target never changes.
- The shared engage tail (brace, aim, throw, shot) is one function used by every role; guest collision prediction only
  counts teammates.
- Camper post (`posts.json camper_posts`): a placeholder, the red 2v2 entry spot beside the red stand, not yet from
  demos.

- **The camper adds about 4.75 captures a world (+66%)** and halves the runner's weapon deaths: the defenders split
  their fire, and the camper kills base D about every 80 s.
- Most camper captures follow a take while the runner was dead; the runner picks up less (15 against 22) because the
  flag is often already gone.
- The control arm sits with the earlier Kejim arms (6.62 against 5.38 and 6.90 captures), so the target-choice change
  shows nothing without a camper; the earlier 5.38 was noise.
- **Found in both arms:** chase D picks up the runner team's flag about 0.4 times a world (`Item: ... team_CTF_blueflag`
  by Chaser_v2): its `bot_chaserGoal` is the runner's stand, where touching the flag takes it. Harmless one-sided; a
  real fault in a two-sided game.

## B1b: camping by situation, not a post (owner, same day)

Owner: campers stay around the flag stand and drift with what is going on: with no pressure they may grab a nearby
weapon; with the base there they move away or fight; with the enemy carrier there they fight it but do not chase it.
No demo derivation. Built as goals after carry, retake and take: `fc` (fight the enemy carrier, following it only
inside `bot_camperLeash`, default 1,000 u from the enemy stand), `fight` (an enemy within `bot_camperPressure`,
default 1,500: stand and fight inside the leash), `rearm` (the same but no stocked weapon: fetch one), `retreat`
(under `bot_camperFightHealth`, default 50: the spot around the stand farthest from that enemy), `camp` (no pressure:
rearm near the stand, else a spot 200-500 u from it nearest where it is). Spots are interior graph cells on the
stand's level. `.codex-research/camper-20261002-b/`, 8 worlds an arm.
