# Planned drop search; item pickups passed by default

Recorded 2026-09-25. Follows the [drop-aim record](2026-09-25-drop-aim.md).
Owner, on that record: aiming a second fixed heading at the takeoff is choosing
between two consequences instead of planning for what the drop is for; item
pickups should simply pass (the negatives are death, pads and lifts). Items
on by default without re-taking the gates; then build the planned search.

## Items on by default

`bot_forecastItems` now defaults on (unset or nonzero; 0 off): weapon, ammo,
armor and health pickups do not refuse a rollout. Hurt volumes, pads, flags,
powerups and holdables still refuse; lift calls were already modelled rather
than refused, and a landing whose fall damage would kill still refuses.
`movement_cost_runner.py` and `movement_decision_explore.py` pin the
production settings block and now pin it to 1. The production library was
explored with items off; the gates were not re-taken (owner).

## The planned drop search

`bot_moveDropPlan` (default on since this record; tested off) replaces the drop edge's two fixed
passes (12 full-speed taps along the edge's heading, then the same at
150 u/s with step offs) with a search built from what the drop is for:

- **Where.** The ledge is sampled on the grid: the takeoff and two cells
  either side, kept where the graph has the upper floor just back from the
  line.
- **Which way.** Across each point along the edge's heading, and along the
  bearing from the bot when that is 5 to 50 degrees off it.
- **How fast.** The lower floor ahead of each line is read from the graph to
  its far end: the first missing or hurt/liquid cell, or where the upper
  floor resumes past the gap (the graph keeps a lower floor's cells under an
  upper one, so without that Metalorn's slots read 512 units of floor). With
  the fall time of a step off and of a tap (`JUMP_VELOCITY`), the approach
  speed is the one that lands 48 units inside that floor. At or above the
  speed the bot carries it runs unbraked; below, one candidate brakes late
  (`bot_moveDropBrake`) and one from the start. Two launches (step off at the
  ledge, tap 24 units before it), so at most 4 candidates a line.
- **Steering.** The plan's `lineRunup` makes the run-up aim a little ahead on
  its line (as the flight follows its line), so a start beside the ledge
  curves onto the crossing the search chose.
- **Which.** Every admitted rollout is scored by its landing frame, plus the
  walk-priced route left from the path cell nearest its landing, less the
  straight search's exit credit (`bot_moveExitSpeed`); lowest wins. A landing
  no path cell follows is not taken.

Smoke: Metalorn edge 179 (the 224-unit slot drop) reads 368 units to the far
lip, commits a 318 u/s tap on its first search (14 of 30 admitted) where the
old search's full-speed pass all landed on the lip and it fell back to a
150 u/s step off; `red_out_L2_stand` class 150, 14,500 to 13,075 ms.

about 700. The score prices the route left at the walk's speed, so it cannot
tell a landing that enters a better strafe line; the straight search has the
same limit. Not repaired.

(`.codex-research/dropplan-20260925-b/`, against the control arm above; the

320, but no case got faster. Withdrawn; the sources are build b's again (all

## Follow-up, 2026-09-26: the walk-priced route left is not the loss

class 50 (control 17,375 ms, planned 18,675), `.codex-research/dropplan-20260925-a`:

- The arms part at the first drop (edge 179), 25 ms after release: the planned
  drop lands faster (486 against 452 u/s) but the next hop commits 150 ms later,
  and the next reroutes install slightly different paths (134 against 132 cells).
- Both arms then bog down in the same place (control 632 to 228 u/s at 5.5-6 s;
  planned 566 to 219). The control's next hop chain crosses the lower ledge about
  250 units west of edge 150 and reroutes onto a 101-cell path; the planned arm
  finds no admissible hop (12 of 12 `slower_route`), walks, and drops at edge 150

  landing the score misjudged.

  divergence between start classes, not a scoring defect.
