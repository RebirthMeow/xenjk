# The level-1 exit gate, rows 1 to 4 on one build

Recorded 2026-09-22. The four measurable rows of the
[level-1 exit gate](../../archive/movement-2026-10-05/PLAN.md#level-1-exit-gate-2026-09-21) measured together
on build `tie-break-build-20260922-a` with the four-key library of
[the tie-break campaign](2026-09-22-tie-break-and-tally.md), in one detached
sequential campaign. No C source was to move and none did. Row 5 is not
measured here.

> **Rows 1, 2 and 3 are met. Row 4 is met in the floor's configuration and not
> in row 2's.**
>
> **Row 2, at the six repetitions the row asks**, over the four declared start
> classes weighted equally: equal-weight median **15,962.5 ms** against the
> row's 16,500, tail (worst completed library world) **16,725 ms** against
> 18,000. The control arm on the same worlds is 18,475 ms and 21,125. All 24
> library worlds selected on the **exact** rung, hit **every entry** of their
> tape with every aim gap 0.0000, and the six worlds of each class agree to the
> millisecond. Declared-weight mean **16,054.6 ms** against 17,427.7.
>
> **Row 3, as the plan re-based it a second time on 2026-09-22**, is met: at no
> class does the library world wait a larger share than its control - 30.4
> against 71.2, 55.1 against 59.5, 37.9 against 37.9, 35.0 against 50.5 - and
> rows 2 and 3 move **together at every class**, both falling at 75, 100 and
> 150 and both flat at 125. No candidate is `admitted` at any waiting frame in
> any of the 32 worlds, which is unchanged.
>
> **Row 1 is met on both floors.** The 36-case floor is **36 of 36 with the
> library off and 36 of 36 with it on**, zero deaths, the movement owner on
> every frame, zero parity mismatches, every world reporting a **non-zero**
> comparison count (419 to 825), and no forbidden spend. Both seeded
> regressions pass with the library on. The library fires in **11 of the 36
> floor cases** - the `phase` rung at the three `red_out_*_stand` cases and the
> `link` rung at eight `red_ret_*` cases - and harms none of them.
>
> **Row 4 has a median and a tail for every one of the twelve level-1 cases**,
> three repetitions per arm, in the floor's unseeded configuration: 72 worlds,
> all complete, zero deaths, the movement owner throughout, one case where the
> library arm's median is slower (`blue_ret_L1_respawn4`, by 200 ms). In row
> 2's own **seeded** configuration the row is **not** met: `red_ret_L1_respawn4`
> and `blue_out_L1_respawn4` **die** - in both arms - so neither has three
> completed repetitions there.
>
> **What failed, and it is not the library.** The 2026-09-16 seeded graph kills
> the bot on those two respawn4 cases. A four-world probe with the seed removed
> and everything else held returns both cases passing in both arms, zero
> deaths, the movement owner. The library is not implicated: the deaths fall on
> control worlds as readily as on library worlds and the library selects no
> tape in either case.
>
> **A second thing the unseeded arm shows**: a tape does not transfer off the
> graph it was explored on. Unseeded, `red_out_L1_stand` selects on the
> **exact** rung and hits **0** of its entries, so the library is inert there
> and the unseeded row-4 arm measures that inertness rather than the library's
> effect.

## What was asked

Measure gate rows 1 to 4 together on `tie-break-build-20260922-a` with the
four-key library bound by hash, in one detached sequential campaign, and write
the result up with a per-row verdict, the binding, what failed and what it does
not establish. Do not change any C source; do not rewrite the plan or the gate
rows.

## What failed

- **The seeded graph kills two level-1 respawn cases.** Eleven worlds of phase
  C ended in `death` at 17,600 ms, all of them `red_ret_L1_respawn4` or
  `blue_out_L1_respawn4`, in **both** arms (six library worlds, five control),
  each with owners `legacy`, `move` and `none` rather than `move` alone. The same two
  cases pass in both unseeded floors and in all three unseeded repetitions of
  each arm. The four-world probe isolates it: seed removed, `--parallel 1`
  kept, library on and off - **all four pass, zero deaths, owner `move`**,
  17,025 / 17,025 / 15,800 / 17,025 ms. So the difference is the 2026-09-16
  seeded graph and not the library, not the parallel form and not the build.
  This is a defect in the research seed's coverage of those two starts and it
  is not repaired here.
- **The seeded configuration also changes ownership on the respawn5 cases**,
  which report `jumppad` beside `move` in all twelve seeded worlds and `move`
  alone in all twelve unseeded ones. They pass in both.
- **One pin miss.** `c100-lib-6` landed no corner level and was retained and
  replaced under `-a2`, which returned the class-100 cell exactly. One of
  33 phase-A worlds.
- **A tape does not survive its graph seed.** Unseeded, an exact-rung selection
  hits zero entries. Nothing in the library's design says a tape is bound to a
  graph cache, and it is.
- **Row 5 is not measured**, so the gate is not reported passed.

## What this does not establish

**This is one map.** `mp/ctf_kejim`, checksum `-1301924053`. Rows 2 and 3 are
one case on it, `red_out_L1_stand`, one leg (`t1e83`) from one start cell, four
of the eight start phases. Rows 1 and 4 cover the fixture's 36 and 12 cases on
the same map and nothing else.

**Contention is out of scope**, as the plan says, and so is row 5: every world
here runs under `--parity 256` with one bot, which is the harness the cost row
requires to be absent. Nothing here prices the search.

**Row 2's six repetitions per cell return one distinct time each.** That is
reproducibility within a pinned class, not a distribution; the gate's
"distribution" is across the four classes, and four points weighted equally is
what the median and the tail are computed from.

**Row 3 is an attribution, not an accounting.** The row has no threshold by
the plan's own second re-basing, and the shares here range from 30 to 55
percent on trips that are 1,600 to 5,175 ms faster. A reader should take from
it that the gain is not a selection artifact, and not that waiting has been
solved: no candidate is admitted at any waiting frame in any world of this
campaign.

**Row 4's two arms measure different things.** The unseeded arm has every
case's number and an inert library; the seeded arm has a live library on one
case and two cases that die. Neither arm alone is the row, and the row is
reported as both.

**The library is four tapes for one leg from one cell.** Eleven of 36 floor
cases form a key at all, and none of those eleven reproduces a tape. The
evidence that the library does not harm the other 35 cases is that both floors
are 36 of 36; it is not evidence that the library helps them.
