## Why full-speed drops fail

Retained worlds of `dropbrake-20260925-a` (96 arm), drop searches only:

| Map | Searches | Full-speed pass fails | Whole search fails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metalorn v2 | 238 | 114 | 3 |
| Carratos | 79 | 73 | 40 |
| Taloraan | 228 | 150 | 62 |
| Dantooine v3 | 138 | 113 | 89 |

Three causes, in the order they matter:

1. **The run-up ignores where the takeoff is.** `PlannedSearch` runs every
   candidate along the edge's heading (plus or minus 15 degrees) from the live
   state, and the executor steers the run-up along that heading
   (`bot2_move.c`, run-up branch of the hop step). The search fires once the
   path is within 400 units of the edge, often from the side: on Taloraan
   edge 38 the takeoff was 268 units ahead along the heading and 169 to the
   side, so every candidate crossed the ledge line well beside a short ledge
   (`miss`, `corridor`, `off_path`). Lateral offset at the search, by outcome:
   all 62 whole failures on Taloraan began 96 units or more off the line, 32 of
   Carratos's 40. On a long ledge the offset run still crosses the same ledge,
   which is why Metalorn mostly survives it.
2. **Item pickups refuse the rollout.** `bot_forecastTriggerLog=1`, class 150,
   build `dropbrake-build-20260925-b` (`.codex-research/droptrig-20260925-a/`):
   on Carratos 495 drop-candidate refusals are one `weapon_demp2` beside the
   stand drop, 68 a rocket launcher, 12 ammo; `trigger_hurt` 298 and
   `trigger_push` 172. On Taloraan `trigger_hurt` (the pit) 666, items 241
   (rockets, flechettes, power cells). Metalorn's pass was lost (below).
3. **Real hazards.** Full-speed flights into Taloraan's pit, and across
   Metalorn's slots to the same level (edge 179: a 224-unit drop landing 30
   units past the ledge; a 520 u/s tap clears it and the walk declines
   `offmesh`). These are correct refusals; the slow pass handles them.

## Changes

- `bot_moveDropAim` (test, default off): a drop search begun
  `MOVE_DROP_AIM_LATERAL` (16) units or more off the ledge line adds one
  heading aimed at the takeoff, its run-up the distance to it, in both passes
  (4 taps full speed; 4 taps and a step off in each slow group). The existing
  candidates are unchanged and the soonest admitted still wins. The commit and
  failure events carry `aim`, `aimed` and `lateral`.
- `bot_forecastItems` already existed (weapon, ammo, armor and health pickups
  do not refuse; flags, powerups and holdables still do). It was set aside on
  Kejim evidence ([trigger classification](2026-09-20-trigger-classification.md));
  measured here on the new maps.

## Results

Build `dropaim-build-20260925-a`, production settings, pinned classes 50 and
150, arms control (both 0), aim, aim+items
(`.codex-research/dropaim-20260925-a/`, `dropaim-20260925-a.sh`; paired table
`pairs.txt`):

| Map | Aim: pairs, sum | Aim+items: pairs | faster | slower | sum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Taloraan | 60, -13,075 ms | 65 | 26 | 25 | **-35,525 ms** |
| Carratos | 65, +525 | 67 | 47 | 10 | **-58,325** |
| Metalorn v2 | 57, -1,400 | 54 | 24 | 0 | **-20,150** |
| Dantooine v3 (L1-2) | 36, +2,925 | 34 | 17 | 12 | **-21,975** |
| Kejim | 60, 0 | 59 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **All** | 278, **-11,025** | 279 | 114 | 47 | **-135,975 ms** |

Zero failures and zero deaths in every arm; incompletes are start-class pin
misses and one unacknowledged runner creation. Worst single loss with
aim+items 1,775 ms (Carratos `blue_out_L1_respawn5`, class 50), best gain
5,700 (Carratos `blue_out_L1_stand`). The maps are mirror-symmetric, so pairs
come in near-identical twins: fewer independent samples than the count.

Whole drop-search failures, control against aim against aim+items: Taloraan
27 / 8 / 12, Carratos 36 / 25 / 24, Dantooine 28 / 18 / 4, Metalorn 2 / 0 / 0
(Carratos's aim+items routes take more drops; its failures are the class-150
arm's).

- **Aim alone** is worth -13 s on Taloraan (the short-ledge map), neutral
  elsewhere. Dantooine's +2,925 at class 50 is three stand cases diverging
  late on the upper level, after an aimed full-speed drop had put the runner
  ahead (`blue_out_L1_stand`: level with the control at 17 s).
- **Items** carry most of the gain and change every rollout, not only drops.
  No items-only arm was run, so the split between the two is by difference.
- **Kejim** route times are identical in both arms, but items change its
  search: forecast refusals 4,256 against 3,488 in the class-150 worlds. The
  gates were taken with the production library, which these floors do not load.

Parity with the harness on (`--parity 256`), aim+items, class 150
(`.codex-research/dropaim-parity-20260925-a/`): Carratos 20,742 frames
compared, Taloraan 18,836, **zero mismatches**; two Carratos refusals, one
mirrored site, `unsupported=1` (solid), not a trigger.
