# Our 4v4 team against legacy Bot2 (2026-10-03)

Owner: "can we do 4 of our bots vs 4 bot2" - legacy Bot2 opponents, measured laps worlds.

## Setup

`defense_laps.py --vs-legacy N`: our team on blue (the runner, `--camper`, and `--roles base,chase` at the blue posts:
the unnumbered `bot_defenderPost`/`bot_chaserPost` cvars and the mine and ambush spots follow the side) against N
`addbot Alora 3 red 0 Legacy<n>_v2` bots. In play mode (`bot_movementOnly 2`) a `_v2` bot that is neither a runner nor
a declared defender runs the full legacy `Bot2_Think`; its role comes from its team rank (offense, chase, offense,
base); its movement goes through the current movement code like every Bot2 bot. The tape library is off, as in the
two-sided worlds. `defense_laps.py` now prints the `defenderpost` reply's POST/REFUSED/RELEASE lines.

One code change: the defenders' `TargetCandidate` (bot2_defender.c) accepted only the enemy runner and the enemy's
declared bots, so base D, chase D and the camper ignored legacy opponents; it now accepts any enemy bot. In the
existing 4v4 every enemy bot already qualified, so B3 behaviour is unchanged.

## Measured

Build `legacy-build-20261003-a`, `.codex-research/legacy-20261003-a/` (`run.sh`, `score.py`), 16 worlds of 600 game s,
Kejim, the B3 common arguments. Per world (standard error):

| | ours (blue) | legacy (red) |
|---|---|---|
| captures | **26.8 (0.7)** | **0.0** |
| enemy-flag pickups | 29.8 (0.7) | 11.0 (1.4) |
| weapon deaths | 8.9 (0.6) | 40.1 (1.7) |
| falls | 5.5 (0.6) | 0.1 |
| self-kills | 16.1 (0.5) | 27.5 (1.3) |

Ours won all 16 worlds. Our self-kills are mostly the runner's capture self-kill (`bot_runnerCapKill`, on in play).
The legacy bots' self-kills are their own rules (ai_bot2.c): a kill once falling to death (so their pit falls show as
suicides, hence about zero falls), a kill right after losing the flag (a routing-test harness left on), and a tactical
kill to restore Force. Legacy carriers picked up our flag 11 times a world and never brought it home.
