# The own flag at the stand: bot_forecastOwnFlag

Recorded 2026-09-24. Found from the owner's [level-2 runs](2026-09-24-human-level2-runs.md):
every human run takes the upper route north from the red stand, and the
level-2 planner's release route is that same route (16 strafe edges along
z 1,808, planned at 14,194 ms). The bot lost it on the first edge.

## Mechanism

A stand start is inside its own flag's pickup box. The forecast refuses any
rollout that touches a trigger (`unsupported=256` on frame 1), so every
candidate from the release frame is refused and the first strafe edge
(50732, takeoff at (163, -1093), about 70 units from the stand) is
`deferred` until the walk leaves the box. At Force Speed, bought at release,
the bot is past that takeoff when judging resumes: the edge is avoided as
`walked`, and every replan takes the southern route to the lower floor.

Natively the touch does nothing: `Team_TouchOurFlag` returns without effect
when the flag is at its base and the toucher carries no enemy flag.
`bot_forecastOwnFlag` (test, default 0; `bot2_forecast.c`) exempts exactly
that flag item from the trigger refusal: the root's own team flag, not a
dropped one, while the root carries no flag. It is part of the replay
buffer's settings key.

The explorer's route branching could not recover the upper route: a required
edge is dropped once passed, only one may be required, and every later replan
returned to the lower floor (`.codex-research/explorer-l2rb-c150-20260924-a/`,
stopped after its route worlds).

## Adopted, 2026-09-24

Owner: default on (unset reads as 1, 0 turns it off); commit 7049d7ae.

**The ctf4 stall was a lift deadlock.** Pinned at class 50 it reproduces
(twice, `.codex-research/ctf4-stall-20260924-a/`): after the drop is refused
the replan rides lift *21 down; the walk brakes onto the call cell's point at
(4532.8, -1077.1), 29.6 units from the edge's takeoff, and the down-ride
starts only within 24 (`MOVE_LIFT_REACH`), so neither moved for 175 s. The
ride now starts within twice the reach or from the takeoff cell, and its call
phase steers onto the takeoff. Build `ownflag-build-20260924-c`: both class-50
worlds call, board, ride down and pass at 33,100 ms.
