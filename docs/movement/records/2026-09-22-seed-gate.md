**Identity.** Each harness-on world was compared with its harness-off world:
the committed sequences over the leg, with counters and `rollout_ms` dropped.
All four stand classes and both pinned regressions are identical, commit for
commit. The first comparison of the regressions (`diag-seeded` against
`seeded-on`) was unpinned, so the two ran from different release levels and
the comparison meant nothing. That is why the pinned `ident-*` pair was run.

**The `jumppad` owner.** The seed's routes from the respawn5 starts cross a
jump pad. Bot2's `Bot2_JumppadMovement` runs ahead of the rewrite in
`Bot2_ExecuteMovementImpl` and owns those frames. The cases pass, and they
report `move` alone on graphs without those routes. This is an upstream yield
of the kind the [floor safety record](2026-09-22-deferred-save-and-floor-safety.md)
lists as remaining. It is not the legacy owner. Whether row 1 admits it is
an owner decision.

## Row 4 and the carry-over check

Every case ran unpinned, so within an arm the times spread with the start
class. The table gives medians, with the three times in brackets.

| Case | Library | Control | Library rung |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `red_out_L1_stand` | 15,975 | 18,025 | exact |
| `red_ret_L1_stand` | **19,500** (19,625 / 19,500 / 19,075) | **18,875** | link, none, link |
| `red_ret_L1_respawn4` | 17,775 | 17,775 (tail 18,775) | link |
| `red_ret_L1_respawn5` | 15,775 (tail 16,525) | 15,775 | link |
| the other eight | - | - | none |

Where the rung is `none`, no tape is selected. Those arms are the same
configuration, and their gaps (for example `blue_out_L1_respawn5` 18,925
against 15,875) are start-class spread only. They are not a library effect.

**Carry-over.** The library selects off its explored cells only on the
red-return legs, on the `link` rung. There it is neutral or harmful:
- `red_ret_L1_stand` is 625 ms slower at the median.
- `red_ret_L1_respawn5` has a worse tail.

A tape used off its cells does not help. The `link` rung is the candidate to
remove.

## Row 5

`tools/movement_cost_runner.py`, four bots, `--parity 0`, deferred save (the
build default), 800 frames. Arms were alternated seed, v3, v3, seed; the seed
arms had the library on.

| World | p50 | p95 | p99 | Worst | Over 5 ms | Over 25 ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| K1-seed | 67 us | 20.3 ms | 100.4 ms | 215.8 ms | 93 | 39 |
| K2-seed | 56 us | 18.5 ms | 98.3 ms | 218.9 ms | 62 | 37 |
| K1-v3 | 71 us | 26.8 ms | 60.5 ms | 107.7 ms | 107 | 48 |
| K2-v3 | 70 us | 29.5 ms | 64.7 ms | 106.4 ms | 113 | 53 |

- Every worst frame is boundary preparation, the landing search.
- The seed has fewer expensive frames than v3, but they cost more: about 1.6
  times at p99 and twice at the worst.
- Its 94,711 edges make each `Graph_PlanState` and reroute call dearer. That
  is the suspect named in the
  [boundary cost attribution](2026-09-22-boundary-cost-attribution.md), and it
  is still unmeasured on the seed.
- The next row-5 work is unchanged: memoise `RerouteFrom` within a search,
  then spread the finish phase across frames. Both must be decision-identical
  on the reference cells.

## What this does not establish

- **That the gate passes.** Row 5 and the row-1 ownership question are open.
- **That rows 2 and 3 hold away from the four declared classes.** The
  library has tapes only for them.
- **The cost of the seed's planning, separately.** The cost worlds report per
  frame, not per call.
