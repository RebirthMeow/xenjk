## Native rule found first

A self-kill does **not** drop the flag in this engine: `player_die` (g_combat.c) returns it to its stand on
`MOD_SUICIDE`. So the hand-off is not a drop at the carrier's feet for the camper's `retake`; it is a return to the
enemy stand, where the camper camps and, its runner dead, runs `take`. The drop-geometry checks the plan asked for
(grounded, on a graph cell, no pit near the drop) have nothing to protect under this rule; only "grounded" is kept.

## What was built (`bot_runnerHandoff`, default 0)

Runner side, ai_bot2_objective.c (`RunnerHandoffPolicy`, `RunnerHandoffFollow`), called from the carrying branch of
`Bot2_MovementOnlyCommand`; the self-kill is `G_Kill`, as CAPKILL does. A carrier kills itself when all hold:

- carrying, alive, grounded, and its straight distance to its own stand is at least `bot_runnerHandoffDepth`
  (default 0.5, the half line) of the sum of its distances to both stands: never near its own capture;
- spent: Speed not running and Force under `bot_runnerHandoffForce` (default 25). In `ce` 93% of carrying samples are
  under 50 Force and 79% under 25, so "cannot buy Speed" alone would fire on almost every carry; 25 is half a purchase
  and 5 s of regeneration short, near the pilot's median carrier self-kill (11 Force, 63 health). Health is not a
  gate, since the pilot's carriers self-kill at a median 63;
- a fresh teammate (the one with the most Force): alive, Force at least `bot_runnerHandoffMateForce` (default 50, can
  buy Speed for the carry) and 25 over the carrier's, health at least `bot_runnerHandoffMateHealth` (default 60). The
  first default, 75, never passed: the camper buys Speed itself and mostly holds 50 (smokes 2-4);
- near: its graph route time (Graph_Path) to the enemy stand at most `bot_runnerHandoffMateMs` (default 3,000), and at
  least `bot_runnerHandoffLeadMs` (default 0) quicker than every enemy the runner perceives (the threat model's
  last-seen positions, not live state). Routes are evaluated only once the cheap gates pass, at most every 250 ms.

Log lines: `[RUNNER] HANDOFF` (inputs: Force, health, both stand distances, mate Force/health, mate and best enemy
route ms), `HANDOFF_SKIP` (rate-limited, the refusing gate), `HANDOFF_OUTCOME` (`taken by=`, `not_taken` after 30 s,
then `captured` or `lost` with `dead=`). Runner movement is unchanged, so the bench floor is not affected.

## Results (per ten-minute world)

| | control (`ce`) | hand-off, depth 0.5 (`ho`) | hand-off, depth 0.6 (`hd`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| hand-offs a world (per world) | - | 1.00 [2,0,0,4,0,1,1,0] | 1.00 [4,0,0,0,1,1,0,2] |
| taken by the camper / captured / lost | - | 8 / 5 / 3 | 7 (+1 at world end) / 1 / 6 |
| runner-team captures (se) | 10.75 (1.29) | 9.00 (1.50); 10.29 without ho-5 | 10.12 (0.77) |
| runner / camper captures | 7.38 / 3.38 | 4.50 / 4.50 | 5.62 / 4.50 |
| runner deaths (self-kills: CAPKILL + hand-off) | 21.8 (7.4) | 20.0 (5.5) | 23.8 (6.5) |
| camper deaths | 18.4 | 14.6 | 17.8 |

(Runner-team captures exclude chase D's touches of the runner team's flag.)

- **The hand-off works as a mechanism**: the camper took 15 of 15 handed flags (one more was pending at world end),
  median 2.1 s after the self-kill.
- **It does not add captures.** 6 of the 15 taken flags were captured; 9 camper carries died, at a median 4.75 s after
  the take, beside the enemy stand. The deeper arm hands off nearer the stand and loses more (6 of 7). Captures move from
  the runner to the camper; team captures sit within noise of the control, if anything lower.
- The cause is the native return: the self-kill throws away the carry already made (on average about a third of the way
  home at depth 0.5) and puts the flag back beside base D, so the fresh carrier starts where the defence is strongest.
  Refusals are mostly `mate_far` (the camper more than 3 s from the stand) and `mate_spent`.
- Default stays **0**.
- **`ho-5` is a stuck world, not a hand-off effect**: the runner stood off the graph (`[MOVE] ... reason=no_path
  from=-1`, x=161 y=975 z=1368) from 45 s to the end, with no hand-off and 0 captures. The control's `ce-4` (3 captures)
  shows no such run.
