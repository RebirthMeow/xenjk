## What a stall is

A **frozen** world: for over a minute every bot stays within 50 u of where it was 10 s earlier (`frozen.py`; the
"every bot below 60 u/s" test missed them). In each one both carriers held the enemy flag on their own stands and each
enemy chase D stood about 100 u from one with a weapon it could not fire; the camper on that side had none; base D
looked at nothing (it targeted the unseen enemy carrier across the map); the runner without a job (its teammate
carrying) stood in mid-map. Game time ran on; nothing changed until the world ended (up to 7.5 minutes).
