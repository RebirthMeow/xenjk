## New facts from the follow-ups

- **Dantooine's replan tail is guided searches**, not a goal's first asks: 354 searches a world reach 4,000
  expansions, 320 of them already guided, about 34 replans a world over 10,000 expansions.
- **Guidance table work was invisible to the work meter** (now `guide_pops` on `[FRAMEWORK]`): 12-15 million
  settled cells a world, up to 41,000-77,000 in one frame, about 11-21 ms. Spreading builds over frames, as route
  tables already are, is open (with BP-6). 2026-11-10: builds spread behind `bot_movePlanGuideSpread` (default 0;
  peak -21% on Kejim, little on Dantooine; folds not spread).
- **CTF standoff**: with both carriers at home the camper replans every frame at a 256 u wall climb that finds
  nothing (2,400 replans a minute in the worst world). `laps_identity.py` needs `--min-seconds` for such worlds.
- **BX-1's reach**: 55 rerouted arrivals in 72 worlds took the flag on a second hop after an off-path landing; 4 of
  them died to the world within 6 s (7.3%, against 2.0-2.8% for other arrivals; small numbers).
