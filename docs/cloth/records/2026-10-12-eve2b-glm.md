# Eve 2B -> JKA GLM (work notes, 2026-10-12) - commercial game assets, kept local

## Recon
- eve_2b.blend: armature `CH_P_EVE_Nier_01_LOD0_Skeleton` (382 bones: 53 `Bip001*` biped, 300 `Ab*`/`Dm*` helpers, Root), ONE mesh `Eve`
  (112,384 verts, 165,162 tris, 14 material slots, 327 vertex groups, 170 shape keys all at 0 except Basis, UV0+UV1).
  A-pose rest, 1.72 m (hair top), X = her left, face toward -Y, Z up, metres. No object transforms.
- Materials (tris): Hair 51,745 (hair-card atlas, alpha = R of ADIR), Upper 34,126, Boots 18,978 (thigh-high boots + headband),
  Head 17,538, Body 14,130, Mouth 8,174, Skirt 5,361, Hidden 2,712 (dropped), SkirtRigid 2,408 (11 bow/ribbon pieces), Teeth 3,922,
  Eye 3,132, Feather 1,824, Glass 596, Brow 516.
- M_Skirt is ONE connected single-layer sheet (98% of faces face outward); there is no separate lining layer in this blend.
- `_humanoid.gla`: 53 bones, 21,376 frames. Model space X = left, Y = back (face -Y), Z up, JKA units (same handedness/axes as Eve).
  Rest pose is an A-pose with splayed legs. Stock kyle/jan: LOD0 ~2.6-3.8k verts, 2.8-4.7k tris; z 3.12 (soles) .. 67.2 (head top); x +-21.4.
- Engine limits used (strictest = stock rd-vanilla): <= 1000 verts and <= 2000 tris (6000 indices) per surface, <= 32 bone refs/surface,
  <= 4 weights/vertex (10-bit), rend2 MAX_G2_BONES 72 (humanoid has 53). xenjk raised SHADER_MAX_VERTEXES to 10000 but the model respects 1000.
  Skin files allow 1280 surfaces. No engine limit forces decimation; the budget variant is a performance choice.
- Add-on (Blender-Jedi-Academy-Tools v2.1.0) imports/exports fine in Blender 5.2.1 unpatched; a copy is in addon/jediacademy (used only for
  verification). The GLM is written by scripts/jka.py (same layout as the add-on's exporter) because the add-on exporter needs an in-Blender
  scene and cannot report per-vertex source indices; the add-on's importer reads the result back (see verification).

## Fit (scripts/build_glm.py)
- x_fit = T_bone(S*x + (0,0,z0)), S = 37.5 JKA units per metre, z0 = 3.12 (sole height of stock models), then per-vertex linear blend over Eve's
  own skin weights (all 382 bones). Each Eve key bone gets a rigid transform: rotation aligns its bone direction (head -> child head) to the
  humanoid bone direction (minimal twist, composed down the chain), translation puts its joint on the humanoid joint (pelvis, 5 spine/neck/head
  joints, clavicles, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles). Feet keep world orientation (heels flat) and are lifted +1.26 u so the
  boot soles land on z = 3.12. Helper/finger/hair/skirt bones are rigid with their parent. Result: head top 67.1 (kyle 67.2), ankles/knees/elbows on
  the humanoid joints, legs splayed like the stock rest pose.
- Weights: every Eve bone -> humanoid bone(s) (bone_mapping.json: 54 core, 284 nearest-core-ancestor, 20 explicit overrides (twist, knee, elbow),
  24 under-override). Twist bones: upper-arm Tw1 -> humerusX, forearm Tw -> radiusX, thigh Tw1 -> femurX; knee/elbow helpers split 50/50 across the
  two joint bones (as the stock *_knee/*_elbow tags do); fingers -> d1 (thumb), d2 (index+middle), d4 (ring+pinky) j1/j2; ALL hair bones -> cranium;
  ALL skirt strands, ribbons and Venter/Hip helpers -> pelvis (thigh-following skirt left to the cloth sim). Merge by summing, keep top 4, drop < 1%,
  renormalise (10-bit quantised, last weight implied).
- Normals: recomputed on the fitted mesh (position-welded, 60 deg crease, area weighted). Winding: glm stores the reverse of Blender CCW; UV v is flipped.

## Surfaces
- Tree like stock: `stupidtriangle_off` (root) -> `hips` -> `torso` -> `head`/`r_arm`/`l_arm`; `r_leg`/`l_leg` under hips. Body split by
  (dominant-bone region x material), then chunked to <= 880 verts (post UV/normal split) / 1800 tris / 31 bones.
  Names: `<region>` (largest chunk), `<region>_<mat>[_n]` e.g. `head_hair_3`, `r_arm_feather`, `torso_glass`.
- Skirt: `skirt_0..3` (M_Skirt) and `skirt_rigid_0..2` (M_SkirtRigid), parent `hips`, shader `skirt_2s` (cull none, single-layer sheet).
- 46 tag surfaces copied verbatim from stock kyle (same bone weights and placement: *r_hand *l_hand *head_eyes/top/front/back/left/right *back
  *chestg *hip_* *uchest/lchest *shldr *torso_cap_* etc.). Dismemberment cap meshes (`*_off`) not made.

## Textures / shaders / skin
- 2048 JPG (q90): body, upper, skirt, boots, head; 512 teeth; 1024 TGA (RLE, alpha): feather, hair (flat 0.82/0.80/0.78 + R-channel mask * 1.6),
  brow; 8x8 flat TGAs: eye, mouth, glass (alpha 0.35). Normal/ORM maps are not used by rd-vanilla and are not shipped.
- shaders/<model>.shader: skirt_2s (cull none), hair/feather (alphaFunc GE128, cull none), brow/glass (blend). model_default/red/blue.skin identical
  (surface,shader per line). sounds.cfg = stock jan (female voice set). icon_* = 128x128 crop from an in-game shot.

## Variants
- `eve2b` (budget): 55 mesh surfaces + 46 tags + 1 root = 102, 39,621 glm verts, 30,483 tris, 1.98 MB glm. Decimated (Blender Collapse, scripts/decimate_eve.py,
  targets_lite.json): Body 14,130->2,400, Upper 34,126->5,500, Boots 18,978->3,499, Head 17,538->3,200, Hair 51,745->4,500, Mouth 8,174->699,
  Teeth 3,922->400, Eye 3,132->500, Feather 1,824->1,200, Glass 300; Skirt, SkirtRigid, Brow untouched. Hidden dropped.
- `eve2b_full`: no decimation (Hidden dropped): 268 mesh surfaces + 46 tags + root = 315, 226,479 verts, 162,450 tris, 11.1 MB glm.
  Loads and renders in rd-vanilla (145 fps on the dev box, single entity), but is ~5x the cost of the budget one for skinning and for G2 trace
  (saber/bullet) checks; not recommended for play.
